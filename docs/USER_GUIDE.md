# User Guide

Welcome to Benevolence! This guide is designed to help you navigate the platform and make the most of its features. We've aimed for a warm, clear, and easy-to-understand experience.

## 1. Logging In

Access Benevolence at your organization's unique URL. Simply enter your credentials and click "Sign In". If you ever forget your password, the "Forgot password?" link on the login page will guide you through the recovery process.

## 2. The Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a high-level overview of your foundation's portfolio. You'll see key metrics, recent activity, and important alerts at a glance.
*   **Multiple Portfolios:** If you have access to more than one portfolio, you can easily switch between them using the selector at the top of the page.
*   **Section Breakdown:** Each section on the dashboard (e.g., Portfolio Value, Recent Grants, Upcoming Pledges) is designed to give you quick insights into different aspects of your foundation's operations.

## 3. Holdings

The "Holdings" section lists all assets within the portfolio.
*   **Viewing:** Browse the list to see asset types, values, and other key information.
*   **Details:** Click on any holding to view its detailed information, including historical performance, related documents, and notes.
*   **Asset Types:** Benevolence manages various asset types, including Equities, Debt instruments, Grants, Program-Related Investments (PRIs), and Mission-Related Investments (MRIs).
*   **Editing Holdings (Admin Only):** With administrative privileges, you can directly edit holding details to ensure data accuracy.

## 4. KPIs & Charts

Visualize your foundation's impact and performance with our dynamic KPI widgets and charts.
*   **KPI Widgets:** These display key performance indicators, such as total giving, grant disbursement rates, or impact metrics.
*   **Chart Types:** Explore data through various visual formats, including:
    *   **Bubble Charts:** Excellent for showing relationships between three variables.
    *   **Heatmaps:** Useful for visualizing data density or performance across categories.
    *   **Timelines:** Track project progress or historical events.
    *   **Trend Lines:** Show performance over time.
*   **Interpretation:** Hover over chart elements for more details. Admins can configure which widgets and charts appear on your dashboard to prioritize what's most important to your team.

## 5. Tax Center

Manage your foundation's tax-related data efficiently.
*   **Contribution History:** View a detailed record of all contributions made.
*   **AGI Deduction Limits:** Understand your Adjusted Gross Income deduction limits and how they apply.
*   **Carryforward Schedule:** Track any carryforward amounts for future tax periods.
*   **Export Data:** Easily export your data for tax preparation. Options include TurboTax TXF, Excel spreadsheets, and PDF summaries.

## 6. Charity Discovery

Find and learn about nonprofits.
*   **Search:** Look up charities by name or Employer Identification Number (EIN).
*   **Ratings:** Access crucial rating information from sources like Charity Navigator (stars), GiveWell, and ProPublica data.
*   **Add to Portfolio:** If you identify a charity for future engagement, you can add it directly to your portfolio from this tool.

## 7. Board Reports

Generate professional PDF reports for your board meetings with a single click.
*   **Report Content:** Each report includes a summary of the portfolio's value, a breakdown of holdings, key performance indicators, and contribution summaries.
*   **Download & Print:** Once generated, you can download the PDF to share or print for your meetings.

## 8. AI Assistant (Ben)

Meet Ben, your AI-powered portfolio assistant! Ben can help you quickly find information and gain insights from your data.
*   **What Ben Can Do:** Ask natural language questions like:
    *   "What is our total portfolio value?"
    *   "Show me our top holdings by sector."
    *   "Summarize our giving this year."
*   **What Ben Cannot Do:** Ben is designed to work with the data within Benevolence. It cannot access external websites or provide general financial advice outside of your portfolio's context.

## 9. Importing Data

If your organization is migrating from Blackbaud or another system, please consult your administrator. They can provide guidance or direct you to the Data Migration Guide for detailed instructions.

## 10. QuickBooks Integration

Streamline your accounting processes by connecting Benevolence to QuickBooks Online.
*   **Connecting:** Navigate to `Settings` > `Integrations` to initiate the connection.
*   **Account Sync:** Sync your chart of accounts to ensure seamless data flow.
*   **Journal Entries:** Export contributions directly as journal entries into QuickBooks.

## 11. Getting Help

*   **Internal Support:** For most questions, please reach out to your designated administrator within the organization.
*   **Technical Issues:** If you encounter technical problems or bugs, please contact your implementation partner.
